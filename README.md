# Nereqo

> Executive Intelligence for Financial Markets.

Nereqo is an autonomous investment intelligence platform that combines mathematics, machine learning, quantitative research, and software engineering to help investors make better decisions.

Our mission is simple:

> Build the world's most trusted autonomous investment intelligence platform.

---

## Vision

Financial markets produce an extraordinary amount of information every second.

Nereqo transforms that information into clear, explainable investment intelligence by combining multiple layers of market analysis rather than relying on isolated indicators or simple trading rules.

The platform is designed to think like an institutional investment team, continuously evaluating probability, risk, market structure, liquidity, macroeconomic context, and quantitative evidence before reaching conclusions.

## Platform Architecture

Nereqo combines multiple layers of market intelligence.
